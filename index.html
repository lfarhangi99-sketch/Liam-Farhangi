SHEET 2 ABOUT & EDUCATION

Built in the shop, drawn on the screen

I'm a mechanical engineering student at Stevens Institute of Technology, and most of what I know about design I learned by making the part first and drawing it second. I spend my week split between the machine shop and the CAD workstation — running mills and lathes on one side, modeling and tolerancing on the other — which is exactly the loop I want to keep running in aerospace: design something, understand how it's actually manufactured, and make it better because of that.

As President & Chief Engineer of Stevens' SAE BAJA Racing Team, I lead the design, manufacturing, and testing of a competition off-road vehicle end to end. As Head TA for a 200-level manufacturing course, I teach GD&T, CAM, and DFM to the next group of students coming through the shop I work in.
